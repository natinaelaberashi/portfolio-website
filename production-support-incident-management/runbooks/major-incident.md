# Major Incident Response Guide

## A. Confirm impact
Identify affected service, users, business process, and start time.

## B. Assign ownership
Nominate an incident owner and establish a clear communication channel.

## C. Stabilize
Prioritize service restoration using approved workarounds or rollback procedures.

## D. Communicate
Provide concise updates covering impact, current status, actions, and next update.

## E. Recover
Validate service health and confirm the affected business process works.

## F. Review
Complete RCA, identify preventive actions, and update the knowledge base.

This is a portfolio simulation, not a production change procedure.
