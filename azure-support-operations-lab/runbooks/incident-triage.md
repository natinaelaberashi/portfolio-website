# Incident Triage Runbook

## 1. Detect

Review the alert or reported incident and record:

- Service
- Priority
- Start time
- User/business impact
- Initial symptoms

## 2. Triage

Check recent monitoring events, dependency status, configuration changes, and known issues.

## 3. Stabilize

Apply an approved workaround when available and communicate impact and status through the normal support process.

## 4. Investigate

Compare the incident with recent events and recurring failure patterns. Capture evidence before making changes.

## 5. Resolve and validate

Confirm the service is healthy, validate the business function, and document the resolution.

## 6. Close

Record the root cause, corrective action, SLA result, and follow-up prevention work.

> This runbook is a portfolio learning artifact, not a production procedure.
